✨ Introduction ✨

Montessori (Maria): (1870-1952) was a pioneer in the Field of Education and was also the First Woman to recieve a degree in Medicine at University of Rome. She was born in Chairavalle, near Ancona in Northern Italy.

From 1898 to 1900, Dr. Montessori was Principal of a school in Rome. She started many new teaching methods which proved very successful.

Dr. Montessori thought that children should be freed to work at their own speed with the teacher acting as a guide rather than a formal instructor. Another of her ideas was that children should choose what they want to learn and work at studies they like. Dr. Montessori died in the Netherlands where she had gone to start a training centre.
